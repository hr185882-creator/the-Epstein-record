# Security Policy

This policy applies to the sanitized public reference repository. Production systems may use additional controls that are intentionally not documented here.

Do not disclose active vulnerabilities, credentials, private personal information, victim-identifying material, sealed records, restricted source packages, or unlawful content through public issues.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner and include the affected file or component, a concise description, safe reproduction steps, potential impact, and whether sensitive data or analytical integrity is exposed.

Urgent reports include exposed credentials, protected personal information, unauthorized record modification, review-gate bypasses, injection vulnerabilities, restricted-file access, and links to illegal or abusive material.

Testing must avoid privacy harm, data destruction, service disruption, credential access, social engineering, and retrieval or redistribution of unlawful material. Stop once sufficient evidence exists to report the issue safely.
