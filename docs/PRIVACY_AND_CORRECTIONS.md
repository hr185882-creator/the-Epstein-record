# Privacy, Corrections, and Contested Claims

## Publication standard

Publication requires more than technical availability. A record must be relevant to a defined public-interest question, lawfully handled, accurately represented, and necessary to support the stated claim.

## Privacy exclusions

The public repository and public product should not publish:

- victim-identifying information not already lawfully and appropriately public;
- home addresses, personal contact details, medical information, or financial identifiers;
- sealed, expunged, illegally obtained, or access-controlled records;
- private-person information included only because of incidental association;
- graphic abuse material or links that facilitate access to it;
- raw allegations presented without attribution, status, and response context.

## Association records

Contact books, calendars, photographs, event attendance, correspondence headers, address records, and travel records may establish association, access, location, or communication. They do not by themselves establish knowledge, intent, participation, or criminal responsibility.

Every association record should identify exactly what the record establishes, what it does not establish, whether identity has been independently verified, whether the record is contemporaneous, whether it is disputed or ambiguous, and why publication serves a defined public interest.

## Corrections process

A correction request should provide the exact page or record identifier, the asserted error, supporting primary records or reliable reporting, the requested correction, and any relevant privacy or safety concern.

Material changes should record the previous wording or state, the new wording or state, the date, and the reason.

## Emergency handling

Credible reports of exposed credentials, victim-identifying data, sealed records, active threats, or unlawful content should trigger immediate temporary restriction while the issue is reviewed.

## Right of response

When a consequential claim concerns a living person or active institution, a materially relevant denial, explanation, legal response, or contradictory record should be represented near the claim when feasible.

## AI-use control

AI-generated extraction, summaries, entity matches, and classifications are provisional until reviewed. Automated output must not independently promote a claim into a verified or adjudicated state.
